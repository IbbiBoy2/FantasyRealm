THE GAME

Enter FantasyRealm Online

FantasyRealm Online is a fantasy massively multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG) set in a world of magic, exploration and extraordinary adventures.

Players create heroes, explore mysterious lands, face powerful creatures and take part in epic quests.

Every adventurer has a different story. Your character is the beginning of yours.

THE CREATORS

PixelVerse Studios

In the FantasyRealm Online project scenario, PixelVerse Studios is a fictional video game development studio specializing in immersive multiplayer role-playing experiences.

To give players greater freedom, the studio envisioned a dedicated character management platform that extends customization beyond the game's original system.

This educational application demonstrates that vision: a place where players can create, personalize and organize their characters in one interface.
