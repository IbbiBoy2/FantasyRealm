Terms of Sale

Information about the educational and non-commercial nature of FantasyRealm.

✦

1. Educational Project

FantasyRealm is a non-commercial educational project developed as part of the Studi Développeur Web et Web Mobile (DWWM) examination.

The application demonstrates character creation, customisation, management and community features in a fictional fantasy gaming environment.

2. No Commercial Sales

FantasyRealm does not currently offer products or services for purchase.

The application does not provide paid subscriptions, payment processing or real-money transactions.

Any fictional items or equipment available for character customisation are part of the demonstration experience and are not sold.

3. Payments and Refunds

No payment is required to use the educational demonstration.

As no purchases or financial transactions are offered, payment, delivery and refund conditions are not applicable to the current version of FantasyRealm.

4. Fictional Company

PixelVerse Studios is a fictional company described in the examination scenario.

It is not presented as a real merchant or contractual seller through this educational application.

5. Future Commercial Features

If commercial services are introduced in the future, appropriate terms of sale will need to be established and published before transactions are offered.

Users will be informed of any applicable purchasing conditions before completing a transaction.

6. Contact

For questions about FantasyRealm, please use the Contact page.
